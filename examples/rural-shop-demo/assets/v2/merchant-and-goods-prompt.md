```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE original transparent pixel-art character-and-items master sheet for a southern Chinese rural seed shop; this sheet will be the common visual reference for later scene and UI assets.
Primary request: Generate a single landscape 3:2 image, ideally 1536 x 1024. Genuine transparent alpha background everywhere between the isolated subjects. This is NOT a finished UI, poster, game screenshot, or illustrated scene.

Composition: The left third contains exactly ONE full-body middle-aged southern Chinese woman shopkeeper, completely visible from hair bun through shoes, in a front-facing three-quarter stance, gentle expression, holding a small cloth seed pouch in both hands. Grey-black hair in a bun; dark teal-green apron, cream sleeves, warm brown blouse, simple dark skirt/trousers and shoes. Keep her wholly inside the left third with generous transparent separation.
The right two-thirds contain exactly SIX separate item icons in a tidy TWO-COLUMN, THREE-ROW grid. Reading row by row:
top-left: bok choy seed paper packet, with a simple leafy-green vegetable emblem and NO text;
top-right: daikon seed paper packet, with a white radish and green leaves emblem and NO text;
middle-left: rice-seed burlap sack with a small visible group of pale golden rice grains;
middle-right: chili seed paper packet with a simple red chili emblem and NO text;
bottom-left: one small blueberry seedling in a plain earthenware pot, green foliage and a few purple-blue berries;
bottom-right: aged compost burlap sack with visible dark soil and a simple leaf emblem, NO text.
Each icon has the same visual scale, same slight view of the top/front, same lighting and same coarse pixel grain. Equal cells, centered isolated objects, clear transparent gutters. No icon crosses a cell boundary, no touching subjects.

Pixel language and intended scale: Think of the whole sheet as a 384 x 256 logical-pixel canvas, enlarged 4x by nearest-neighbor. The woman is approximately 96 x 176 logical pixels, while EACH item icon is approximately 32 x 32 logical pixels; keep the icons substantially smaller than the full-body woman. Large clean square pixel clusters, sparse purposeful details, firm stepped silhouettes. Draw both the woman and objects in EXACTLY the same pixel-art grammar: no portrait painting combined with tiny sprites. Maintain readable silhouettes rather than decorating surfaces with microtexture. The six icons should look practical at 32 x 32, not like detailed high-resolution product illustrations.

Shared color family: warm paper #f5e7bb / #fff4d2 / #d7bd84; dark ink outlines #2e3b32 / #1e2b28; foliage #537d53 / #86a75c / #aec879; dark teal #3c7b75 / #68a397; wood/burlap browns #6e4731 / #996441 / #c68b4e; skin #9e6451 / #d29c71 / #edbd87; clay/chili #b65349 / #dc8061 / #bd704c; restrained purple #66546c / #948073; earth #4b3c34 / #6a4e40 and muted blue #567788. Use this limited family across all subjects, with at most two or three clearly separated shades per material. It is a color-direction target, not a request for gradients.

Lighting: consistent upper-left key light, two/three flat shade steps, selective dark colored outline. No cast floor shadow, no soft ambient shadow or glow. 
Avoid: any text, letters, numerals, watermark, grid lines, borders, UI frame, labels, background color, checkerboard painted into the image, floor, shop scenery, props other than the requested held pouch and six item icons, extra people or duplicate goods. No photographic paper grain, fabric threads, painterly gradients, smooth shading, antialiasing, blur, subpixel edges, stipple noise or scattered specks. New original designs only; do not imitate or copy a commercial game's character or asset.
```
