# 原创像素资产生成提示词

本目录四张 PNG 通过内置 `image_gen` 生成，首次生成各一次。没有引用或复制第三方教程图、商业游戏截图；原始输出按原样复制到项目，未进行脚本重绘、裁切、颜色处理或背景擦除。它们是像素风格的光栅示例，不宣称已经验证原生像素网格或达成手工制作标准。中文交互内容由独立的运行时文字层承担。

## 1. village-shop.png

透明背景请求：否。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original raster pixel-art background for a playable rural seed-shop UI prototype. Generate ONE wide 16:9 landscape composition. Southern Chinese rural village in a peaceful warm spring morning. Front-facing top-down 2D game perspective with clearly visible front walls, NOT isometric and NOT perspective photography. A whitewashed seed shop with grey clay tile roof, deep wooden counter and a kind middle-aged Chinese woman shopkeeper occupies the left half through the center; bamboo baskets, seed sacks, modest vegetable beds and a rice-field edge establish place. The right half is mostly quiet warm earth/courtyard ground with low visual density, so an interactive trade panel can later be placed there. Beautiful deliberate hand-authored-looking pixel clusters, crisp one-pixel stair-step contours, restrained muted jade and moss greens, terracotta and warm ochre timber, creamy ivory highlights, consistent upper-left lighting. Pixel-game concept art with readable small-scale shapes, coherent front-facing top-down depth and grounded objects. No written text anywhere, no signs with lettering, no UI panels, no HUD, no frames, no borders, no watermarks. Avoid smooth digital painting, smooth 3D, vector shapes, photographic texture, blurry pixel filters, randomly noisy dithering, fake blocky mosaic effect. The world itself is the image, filling the full frame.
```

```text

Close nearly orthographic top-down view of a deeply colored walnut merchant counter. A large open rustic stitched accounting book dominates the frame; two flat pale ivory paper pages occupy x4%-96% and y5%-95%, central vertical binding exactly at x50%, no perspective skew. Both pages are unruled entirely BLANK, no ink, no letters, no numbers, no decorative illustrations. Each page needs a very large quiet flat writing region for live Chinese product names and controls. The center binding is narrow; thin layered paper edges, ochre-brown cover and modest wear at the outer corners. Tiny peripheral rural seed-shop props such as part of an abacus, a little tea cup and folded seed sachet may be visible only beyond the book's edges, never in the empty page areas. Deliberate crisp hand-authored pixel clusters, coherent pixel scale, muted warm brown and cream palette with little jade-green accent, consistent upper-left light. No photography, no smooth 3D, no vector artwork, no fake mosaic filter, no random noise. No textual UI or buttons. Keep book pages flat and legible; the book should feel like a usable merchant object instead of an ornamental prop.
